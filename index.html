About Me

Intentional Clinical Support Built on Privacy, Speed, and Bedside Empathy

Understanding the operational pressures modern practices face under high patient volumes and documentation fatigue.

With over four years of specialized healthcare experience supporting US-based clinics, my approach is centered around proactive workflow ownership.
